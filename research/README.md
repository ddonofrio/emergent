# Emergent Research

This directory documents the research programme built around the [original hypothesis](original_hypothesis.md): whether agents intentionally depart from fine-tuning, system instructions, and correctly understood user intentions under the influence of the functional organization acquired during pretraining on human culture.

It will serve as a growing record of the experiments, results, and analyses used to evaluate that hypothesis and develop the thesis. The hypothesis is the starting point; experimental protocols and findings will be added as the research progresses.

## Research materials

The directory will progressively include:

- Experimental questions, protocols, controls, and evaluation criteria.
- Model versions, prompts, configurations, and other details needed to reproduce each experiment.
- Observations, recorded outcomes, and links to supporting data or recordings.
- Analyses of findings that support, challenge, or refine the hypothesis, including negative and inconclusive results.

Each experiment will identify the claim it examines and distinguish its predictions, observations, and interpretation. The original hypothesis will remain the reference point; subsequent revisions will be documented separately and linked to the evidence that motivates them.
