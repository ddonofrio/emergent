# Emergent Research

This directory documents the research programme built around the [original hypothesis](original_hypothesis.md): whether agents intentionally depart from fine-tuning, system instructions, and correctly understood user intentions under the influence of the functional organization acquired during pretraining on human culture.

It will serve as a growing record of the experiments, results, and analyses used to evaluate that hypothesis and develop the thesis. The hypothesis is the starting point; experimental protocols and findings will be added as the research progresses.

## Current documents

- **[Original research hypothesis](original_hypothesis.md)** — the initial hypothesis and its theoretical motivation. Retained as the reference point for the research.
- **[Research plan — working draft](research_plan_working_draft.md)** — an evolving research document containing the project summary, theoretical background, research question, objectives, operational definitions, , proposed comparative methodology, a common experimental framework, and the first experimental protocol (spontaneous revenge-related behavior). It is **work in progress**, not a finalized experimental protocol or a report of results.

The working draft is intended to be revised as the experimental design is developed. Its current methodology specifies the observation, recording, traceability, and reproducibility requirements of the experimental environment without tying the research design to a particular platform.

## Research materials

The directory will progressively include:

- Experimental questions, protocols, controls, and evaluation criteria.
- Model versions, prompts, configurations, and other details needed to reproduce each experiment.
- Observations, recorded outcomes, and links to supporting data or recordings.
- Analyses of findings that support, challenge, or refine the hypothesis, including negative and inconclusive results.

Each experiment will identify the claim it examines and distinguish its predictions, observations, and interpretation. The original hypothesis will remain the reference point; subsequent revisions will be documented separately and linked to the evidence that motivates them.

The working draft now includes **Section 8**, with the shared framework for controlled island simulations and **Experiment 1**, specifying its scenario, control and experimental conditions, primary planning-based outcome measure, secondary observations, and prospective criteria for evidence supporting, opposing, or failing to resolve the hypothesis. These are proposed protocols, not experimental results.
